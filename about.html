Since 2019

Our story

North Star Bakery began in 2019 when Mira Chen and Jonah Hale rented a narrow shop on Lark Street, installed a used deck oven, and started mixing dough at 3 a.m. The aim was simple: a bakery that neighbors could walk to, with bread that still felt handmade.

Behind the bench

This short clip shows how we fold a country loaf. If the video will not play, visit the shop any morning after seven and you can watch from the counter window.

How we source

Flour comes from a regional mill that still stones a portion of its wheat. Butter is churned at a creamery forty minutes north. Fruit for tarts follows the Saturday market: rhubarb in spring, berries in July, apples once the nights cool.

We buy whole, not pre-mixes. That keeps flavor in the loaf and money in the same county. If a crop is short, we change the menu rather than reach for a substitute that does not taste like this place.
